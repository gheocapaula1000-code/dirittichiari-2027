Your benefits in Italy

Find out which bonuses and public benefits you may be able to get in Italy: Assegno di Inclusione (ADI, income support), Assegno Unico (monthly child benefit), bonus sociali (discounts on bills), Carta Dedicata a te, bonus nido (daycare bonus), rent support. Frequently asked questions

Which benefits can I find with DirittiOk?

You can see, with their limits and links to official sources, the Assegno di Inclusione (ADI), the Assegno Unico (child benefit), the bonus sociali for electricity, gas, water and TARI (waste tax), the Carta Dedicata a te, the bonus nido (daycare bonus), the bonus nuovi nati (newborn bonus) and rent support. Each benefit has other requirements besides the ISEE: you can find the details on the Rights and benefits page.

Do I need to sign up? Where is my data kept?

No, you do not need to sign up. Your ISEE estimate, the money left to live on and the checklist stay only on this phone or computer. We do not track you. If you write to us using the contact form, we receive only the information you type.

Which years count for the ISEE 2027?

Income from 2025, which means the income on your CU 2026 or 730/2026. Assets on 31 December 2025: bank accounts, investments, homes. Rent: the rent in your registered contract, up to 7,000 € a year. This is set by DPCM 159/2013 (Articles 4 and 5): the ISEE looks at the year two years before the DSU is submitted.

Is the app free?

Yes, DirittiOk is free. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not track you. Your calculations stay on your device.

Ready to find your benefits?

Start with the estimate: it is free and your data stays on your phone. Then you can install the app to have it one tap away.
