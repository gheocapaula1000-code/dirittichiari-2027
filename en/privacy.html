Privacy

DirittiOk (DirittiChiari 2027) is a service by Pi.Gi Service di Gheoca Paula (data controller). For any question: info@pigiservice.com.

Your calculations stay on your device

The ISEE estimate, the money left to live on, the benefits and the checklist are calculated and saved only on this phone or computer, locally. We do not send them to any server, we do not ask you to sign up, we do not track you and we do not use artificial intelligence.

You can delete everything at any time with «Delete my data», at the bottom of the page.

Contact form

If you write to us using the contact form, we receive only what you type: your name, email, phone or business (if you add them), your message, your consent and the page you are writing from. We use this data only to reply to you. It never includes any data from your ISEE estimate or the guided steps.

Your message goes to info@pigiservice.com. We keep it for as long as we need to reply to you and to meet any legal obligations.
