How to lower your ISEE legally: what really counts

Please note: This is a rough estimate based on the rules known in November 2026. Official values come only from the DSU (the ISEE declaration) submitted to INPS or a CAF (tax assistance centre). Some 2027 amounts are still estimates.

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · sources checked on INPS, ARERA, MEF.

Money left to live on and the ISEE are two different things

Money left to live on is what you really have left each month. The ISEE instead looks at 2025 income and assets on 31/12/2025: bills, transport, loan repayments and insurance do not lower it.

Allowed choices that make a difference

  • Register your rental contract and keep the registration details to hand: only then is the rent taken off your income (up to 7,000 € a year, +500 € for each child after the second).
  • Enter the mortgage still owed on 31/12/2025 for each property: it is taken off the value of the home. Ask your bank for a certificate of the remaining debt.
  • Home you own: in the ISEE for family and inclusion benefits, the allowance is 91,500 € (120,000 € in the main cities of metropolitan areas), +2,500 € for each child living with you after the first. INPS calculates this automatically: you do not need to do anything.
  • Government bonds, postal savings bonds and postal savings books are left out up to 50,000 € in total for the household (DPCM 13/2025). Declare them anyway: the calculation applies the exclusion.
  • Bank accounts: the higher amount counts, either the balance on 31/12 or the yearly average balance. Check both carefully on your bank statement.
  • Disability: declare any certified medium or severe disability or non-self-sufficiency. Each person adds 0.5 to the scale, and tax-free benefits linked to disability do not count as income.
  • Use the pre-filled DSU on the INPS website: it reduces mistakes and mismatches that can block benefits.

When the current ISEE is worth it

  • You lost a permanent job or your hours were cut, your fixed-term contract ended (at least 120 days in the last 12 months) or you closed your self-employed business.
  • A public benefit stopped (for example NASpI, unemployment benefit).
  • Your income indicator dropped by more than 25% compared with the one in your standard ISEE.

First you need a valid standard ISEE. If you update only your income, the current ISEE lasts 6 months. From 1 April you can also update your assets, if they changed by more than 20%.

What you must never do

  • Do not leave out household members, accounts, investments or properties: the data is cross-checked with the Agenzia delle Entrate (tax agency) and INPS.
  • Do not move your official residence on paper only, to split the household.
  • Do not put assets in other people's names or hide them: you would lose the benefits, a false DSU is a criminal offence and you must pay back what you received.

For special cases (separation, children not living with you, inheritance, businesses), go to a CAF or a patronato (free advice office for workers and families).
