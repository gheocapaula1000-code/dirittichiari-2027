ISEE 2027 bonuses: public benefits, limits and sources

Please note: This is a rough estimate based on the rules known in November 2026. Official values come only from the DSU (the ISEE declaration) submitted to INPS or a CAF (tax assistance centre). Some 2027 amounts are still estimates.

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · sources checked on INPS, ARERA, MEF.

Each benefit has other requirements (residence, citizenship, age, assets). Open the official source before you apply. How we check the values is explained in Sources and rules used and in Method and sources.

Assegno di Inclusione (ADI)

Verified

Limit: ISEE for family benefits up to 10,140 €
What it offers: Tops up household income to a maximum of 6,500 € a year × ADI scale (8,190 € if the household is made up of people aged at least 67, or of people over 67 and family members with a severe disability or who are not self-sufficient), plus up to 3,640 € a year if you rent (1,950 € for the same households).
Who can apply: Households with at least one person under 18, a person with a disability, a person aged at least 60, or someone in the care of social and health services.

Source: INPS, scheda Assegno di Inclusione (information page)

Assegno Unico (child benefit)

2026 value, to be confirmed for 2027

Limit: Full amount up to 17,468.51 €; minimum amount from 46,582.71 €
What it offers: From 58.30 € to 203.80 € a month for each child under 18

Source: INPS, Circolare n. 7 del 30/01/2026 (Assegno Unico 2026)

Bonus sociali for electricity, gas, water and TARI

Verified (updated every three years from 2026)

Limit: ISEE up to 9,796 € (20,000 € with 4 or more dependent children)
What it offers: Automatic with the DSU

Source: ARERA, delibera 2/2026/R/com (bonus sociali)

Carta «Dedicata a te»

Verified

Limit: Standard ISEE up to 15,000 €
What it offers: 500 € a year, one list for 2026-2027

Source: INPS, Carta «Dedicata a te» 2026-2027

Carta Acquisti

2026 value, updated every year

Limit: ISEE up to 8,230.81 €
What it offers: Children under 3 and people aged 65 and over

Source: MEF, Carta Acquisti: 2026 limits

Bonus asilo nido (daycare bonus)

2026 value, to be confirmed for 2027

Limit: Full amount with an ISEE for minors up to 40,000 €
What it offers: 3,600 € for children born from 2024; 3,000/2,500/1,500 € for children born earlier

Source: INPS, Circolare n. 29 del 27/03/2026 (bonus asilo nido)

Bonus nuovi nati (newborn bonus)

Confirmed for 2026, to be confirmed for 2027

Limit: ISEE up to 40,000 €
What it offers: 1,000 € one-off payment

Source: INPS, scheda Bonus nuovi nati (information page)

Rent support (from your Comune or Region)

Check the call for applications

Limit: Each call for applications has its own limits
What it offers: In Veneto: Fondo affitti through local council calls for applications

Source: Regione del Veneto, Fondo affitti (rent fund)

Which ISEE you need for the Assegno Unico

For the Assegno Unico, the ISEE for family and inclusion benefits is used, not the standard ISEE. It is the indicator set by L. 199/2025 and it is usually more favourable if you own your home or have at least two children. Without the Assegno Unico already received, it is also used for the bonus nido and the bonus nuovi nati.

Since 2026, INPS calculates this ISEE automatically. It is used only for ADI, Supporto per la formazione e il lavoro, Assegno Unico, bonus nido and bonus nuovi nati. For everything else (bonus sociali, Carta «Dedicata a te», many local council services), the standard ISEE applies.

Which ISEE is used? Since 2026, INPS also calculates the ISEE for family and inclusion benefits (L. 199/2025). It is more favourable if you own your home or have at least 2 children. It is used only for ADI, Supporto per la formazione e il lavoro (training and work support), Assegno Unico, bonus nido and bonus nuovi nati. For everything else, the standard ISEE applies.

2027 Budget (under discussion)

The 2027 budget bill (A.C. 3174) is being examined by Parliament and is not law yet. Some measures and limits linked to the ISEE could change: we will check them again when the text becomes law.

Official source: A.C. 3174 page on the Senate website.
