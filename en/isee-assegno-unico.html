ISEE and Assegno Unico: how the monthly amount changes

Please note: This is a rough estimate based on the rules known in November 2026. Official values come only from the DSU (the ISEE declaration) submitted to INPS or a CAF (tax assistance centre). Some 2027 amounts are still estimates.

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · sources checked on INPS, ARERA, MEF.

The Assegno Unico e Universale (AUU) is the monthly payment from INPS (Istituto nazionale della previdenza sociale, Italy's national social security institute) for dependent children, until they turn 21 if the required conditions are met. This page explains how the ISEE (the official measure of a household's financial situation) affects the monthly amount, using the limits and values already published by INPS. It does not replace your application to INPS and it does not grant any entitlement: you still need the DSU (the ISEE declaration), when required, and the other requirements set by law.

What the Assegno Unico is

The Assegno Unico e Universale is an INPS benefit for dependent children. If you meet the requirements, you can get it even without an ISEE: in that case you get the minimum amount. For dependent children under 18, the allowance is due under INPS rules (for newborns, according to INPS, it starts from the seventh month of pregnancy). For dependent children aged 18 or over, it can be paid until they turn 21 if the child's total income is under 8,000 € a year and they are in at least one of these situations: they are in school or vocational training, or on a degree course; they are doing an internship or working; they are registered as unemployed and looking for work with the public employment services; or they are doing universal civil service (servizio civile universale). For dependent children with a disability there is no age limit. The full amount and the supplements depend on the ISEE and on other conditions. You apply to INPS: this page and the calculator do not apply on your behalf.

Which ISEE is used

The standard ISEE is not used for the Assegno Unico. What is used is the ISEE per prestazioni familiari e inclusione (ISEE for family and inclusion benefits, L. 199/2025), which INPS has calculated automatically since 2026. It is usually more favourable if you own your home or have at least two children: the home allowance is higher (91,500 €, or 120,000 € in the main cities of metropolitan areas) and the extra points on the equivalence scale are different.

An example, without made-up amounts: in the same family, with the same income and the same home they own, the standard ISEE and the ISEE for family benefits can be different: the second one is usually lower. The Assegno Unico looks at this second indicator, minus any Assegno Unico already received, so last year's payments do not on their own reduce the new amount. The same ISEE, minus the Assegno Unico, is also used for the bonus asilo nido (daycare bonus) and the bonus nuovi nati (newborn bonus). For the bonus sociali (bill discounts), the Carta «Dedicata a te» and many local council services, the standard ISEE still applies.

Limits and amounts (2026 values)

The amounts below come from the Circolare INPS n. 7 del 30/01/2026 (INPS circular). They are 2026 values. The 2027 values are raised in line with inflation at the start of the year: until INPS publishes the new figures, please read them as estimates.

  • Full basic amount: 203.80 € a month for each child under 18, if the ISEE for family benefits (minus the Assegno Unico) is up to 17,468.51 €.
  • Minimum basic amount: 58.30 € a month for each child under 18, with an ISEE of 46,582.71 € or more.
  • Between these two limits, the basic amount goes down gradually, with no sudden jumps. These are basic amounts, without supplements. According to INPS, supplements may apply, among other cases, for children after the second (from the third child onwards), for households with four or more children, for children under one, for children aged one to three in households with three or more children (with an ISEE up to the maximum limit), for disability, for mothers under 21, and when both parents have employment income. The amounts of these supplements depend on the ISEE and on INPS rules: we do not recalculate them in detail here.

We do not give examples with made-up amounts in between: for an estimate for your household you can use the calculator, which always gives only a rough guide.

What happens without an ISEE

If you do not submit the DSU (dichiarazione sostitutiva unica, the ISEE declaration) and so you do not have a valid ISEE, the Assegno Unico can still be granted, but at the minimum amount (58.30 € a month for each child under 18, 2026 value). You do not have to submit an ISEE to get the minimum amount; you need one if you want a higher amount, when you qualify for it under INPS rules.

Frequently asked questions

Which ISEE is used for the Assegno Unico?

It is the ISEE per prestazioni familiari e inclusione (ISEE for family and inclusion benefits, L. 199/2025), not the standard ISEE. INPS has calculated it automatically since 2026. It is taken minus any Assegno Unico already received. The same indicator, minus the Assegno Unico, is also used for the bonus nido and the bonus nuovi nati. For the bonus sociali and many other benefits, the standard ISEE still applies.

What happens if I do not submit an ISEE?

The Assegno Unico can be granted even without an ISEE, but at the minimum amount (58.30 € a month for each child under 18, 2026 value). For a higher amount you need a valid ISEE, as well as the other requirements set by law.

Are the 2027 amounts already official?

No. The amounts and limits on this page are those of the Circolare INPS n. 7/2026 (2026 values). The 2027 values are raised in line with inflation at the start of the year. Until INPS publishes them, please treat them as estimates.

Is the amount the same for each child?

The basic amount (from 58.30 € to 203.80 € a month, 2026 values) is calculated for each child under 18. For dependent children aged 18 or over, it can be paid until they turn 21 if they meet the INPS conditions (study or training, an internship or work, registered unemployment with the public employment services, or universal civil service, with total income under 8,000 € a year); the amount may be different. INPS supplements may be added, including for children after the second (from the third child onwards). This page and the calculator show the basic amount, without supplements.
