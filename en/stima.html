ISEE 2027 calculator: free online estimate

This calculator guides you through 5 steps to estimate your ISEE 2027: the standard ISEE, the ISEE for family and inclusion benefits and, if useful for you, the current ISEE (ISEE corrente). You enter your household, your 2025 income, your fixed costs today and your assets on 31 December 2025. At the end you see an estimate of the indicator, how much you have left each month to live on and which public benefits you could look into, with a link to the source of each limit.

The steps are free and you do not need to sign up. Your calculations stay only on this phone or computer: we do not send your estimate data to any server. Official values come only from the DSU (the ISEE declaration) submitted to INPS or a CAF (tax assistance centre). This is a rough estimate, based on the rules known in November 2026.

The five steps cover: your household; your 2025 income and what comes in today; home and fixed costs; your assets; and any drop in income, for the current ISEE. Before you go to a CAF, you can also check the checklist of documents for the DSU and the page on bonuses and public benefits linked to the ISEE.
