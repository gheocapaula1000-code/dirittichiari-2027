Method and sources

DirittiOk (DirittiChiari 2027) is a service by Pi.Gi Service. It gives a rough estimate of your ISEE, of how much you have left to live on and of public benefits, so you can understand them calmly before you go to a CAF or the INPS website.

How we make the estimate

The calculations follow the rules of DPCM 159/2013 and later updates, including law 199/2025 on the ISEE for family and inclusion benefits. The limits and figures come from official sources: INPS, ARERA, Ministero del Lavoro (Ministry of Labour), Ministero dell'Economia e delle Finanze (Ministry of Economy and Finance), Normattiva.

Every value used in the calculator has a status: verified, 2026 value to be confirmed for 2027, or to be confirmed. The full list, with a link to each source, is on the Sources and rules used page.

What this estimate is not

It is not a DSU and it does not replace the official INPS certificate, a CAF or professional advice. Your calculations stay only on your device. When a law is still being discussed in Parliament, we say so on the page and check it again when it becomes law.

Who runs it

Author of the service: Pi.Gi Service. If you have a question: contact form or info@pigiservice.com.
